# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as members, contributors, and maintainers of the **Secret-FF-Doomsday-Project**, aim to maintain a project environment that supports collaboration, security, productivity, and the interests of the project.

Everyone participating in the project is expected to follow this Code of Conduct and respect the authority of the repository owner and authorized maintainers.

## Our Standards

Examples of acceptable behavior include:

- Treating other contributors respectfully.
- Communicating professionally when discussing project matters.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Respecting the project's rules, decisions, and security requirements.
- Giving appropriate credit to other people's work.
- Protecting confidential information, credentials, and project resources.
- Cooperating with reasonable instructions from the repository owner and maintainers.

Unacceptable behavior includes, but is not limited to:

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted abuse.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spamming, trolling, or deliberately disrupting project activities.
- Plagiarism or falsely claiming someone else's work.
- Malicious code, sabotage, or deliberate damage to the project.
- Unauthorized access attempts or deliberate security breaches.
- Stealing or exposing credentials, private keys, or confidential data.
- Retaliating against someone who reports misconduct.
- Repeatedly violating project rules or deliberately interfering with maintainers' work.

## Responsibilities of Maintainers

The repository owner and authorized maintainers are responsible for managing the project, enforcing its rules, and protecting its security and integrity.

They may review, accept, reject, edit, or remove contributions and may restrict access to project-controlled resources where authorized.

Contributors are expected to follow reasonable project requirements and comply with applicable GitHub policies.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project-related activities, including:

- GitHub Issues.
- Pull Requests and code reviews.
- GitHub Discussions.
- Commits and contributions to the source code.
- Project-related communication channels.
- Other spaces where individuals represent the project.

Conduct outside these spaces may also be considered when it directly affects the project's security, operations, or community.

## Reporting Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the repository owner or an authorized maintainer.

**Repository:** https://github.com/profeinty/Secret-FF-Doomsday-Project

Please do not publish sensitive reports publicly. Reports should include relevant information, such as links, dates, screenshots, and a description of the incident, when available.

Reports will be reviewed at the discretion of the repository owner and maintainers. Retaliation against individuals who make good-faith reports is prohibited.

## Enforcement

The Secret-FF-Doomsday-Project maintains a strict enforcement policy. The repository owner and authorized maintainers have broad discretion to manage participation and protect the project.

### 1. Immediate Permanent Ban

Serious violations may result in an **immediate permanent ban without prior warning**.

Examples include:

- Harassment, credible threats, discrimination, or targeted abuse.
- Doxxing or exposing another person's private information without permission.
- Malicious attacks against the project or its contributors.
- Unauthorized access attempts or deliberate security breaches.
- Introducing malicious code intended to steal data, credentials, or sensitive information.
- Deliberately damaging, sabotaging, or disrupting the project.
- Retaliating against individuals who report misconduct.
- Repeated or severe violations of project rules.

A warning is not required before a permanent ban is imposed.

### 2. Owner and Maintainer Discretion

**The repository owner and authorized maintainers reserve the right to permanently ban any individual from participating in the Secret-FF-Doomsday-Project at their discretion.**

A ban may be imposed when the owner or a maintainer determines that an individual's behavior, attitude, actions, continued participation, or relationship with the project is undesirable or incompatible with the project's interests.

This discretion includes situations in which the owner or a maintainer personally dislikes an individual or no longer wishes to work with them.

A permanent ban may be issued immediately, without a warning, a formal disciplinary process, or a requirement to establish a security violation.

The owner and maintainers are not obligated to provide a detailed explanation for every discretionary ban, although they may choose to do so.

This authority is intended to allow project leadership to manage participation, resolve conflicts, protect working relationships, and determine who may contribute to the project.

### 3. Zero-Tolerance Policy

The project applies a zero-tolerance approach to serious misconduct and deliberate actions that threaten its security, integrity, or operations.

Depending on the circumstances, enforcement actions may include:

- Immediate permanent exclusion from the project.
- Removal or rejection of contributions.
- Restriction of participation in project spaces.
- Revocation of access to project-controlled resources where authorized.
- Reporting relevant security incidents to GitHub or the appropriate authorities when necessary.

### 4. Other Violations

Less severe violations may result in a warning, removal of inappropriate content, temporary restrictions, or permanent exclusion.

The owner and maintainers may decide the appropriate response based on the circumstances. They are not required to follow a progressive warning system.

### 5. Final Decisions

Enforcement decisions are made by the repository owner and authorized maintainers.

Decisions will generally be treated as final within the project. The owner and maintainers reserve the right to review, modify, reverse, or uphold an enforcement decision at their discretion.

All actions remain subject to applicable GitHub policies and platform controls.

### 6. Appeals

Banned individuals may privately request a review of their ban.

An appeal does not guarantee reinstatement, and the existing ban remains in effect while any review is pending.

The repository owner or authorized maintainers may accept or reject an appeal at their discretion.

## Security and Responsible Conduct

All contributors must:

- Never commit passwords, API keys, access tokens, private keys, or other confidential credentials.
- Store sensitive configuration values securely.
- Avoid unauthorized access to accounts, systems, and resources.
- Report vulnerabilities responsibly in accordance with `SECURITY.md`.
- Respect the privacy and intellectual property rights of others.
- Avoid malicious activity that could compromise the project or its users.
- Rotate exposed credentials immediately if they are accidentally published.

Security violations involving intentional harm or unauthorized access may result in an immediate permanent ban.
