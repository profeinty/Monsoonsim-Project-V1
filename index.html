FF live bookmark

One click in your MonsoonSIM tab starts the FF game reader. The bookmark always loads the newest reader from this site, so you never have to remake it when the reader changes. It only reads the game until you turn on Auto-click or start the bot.

On a computer

Drag the one for your mode to the bookmarks bar (press Ctrl+Shift+B if you can't see the bar).

On a tablet or phone

  1. Tap a copy button below.
  2. Bookmark any page, then edit that bookmark: name it as shown and paste over its address.
  3. On the game tab, open your bookmarks (Safari) or type the name in the address bar (Chrome) and tap it.

Using it

Open the calculator, press Go live so the game opens linked to it, then click the bookmark on the game tab. The FF panel opens on the game: drag it by its orange title bar, switch tabs for Today, Stores, Stock and Checks, and press Alt+F to hide or show it. On its own it only reads the game.

Bot (Bot tab, off until you press Start): press Check the screens, then tick Practice and press Start to see what it would do without sending anything. With Practice off, Start opens the calculator, takes its plan for the day and does it: B2B deals, shop and warehouse space, raw materials, store stock and marketing, each within its budget. Its Stop button, the Stop bar or Esc stops it. Machines and prices are still yours to do in the game.

Auto-click (Auto tab, off until you turn it on): press Pick a button, click the game buttons you want pressed, set the wait, then tick Auto-click. It presses them in order and repeats. The red Stop bar or Esc stops it. It only presses buttons; it never types into fields.

If nothing happens, the game page may block outside scripts. Use the Console way in the calculator instead (Copy game reader, paste in the Console).
